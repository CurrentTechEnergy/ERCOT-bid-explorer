Spread between the tails

How far apart the expensive and cheap hours are: the spread a battery or a shiftable load can capture. The 99th percentile price minus the 1st, the 95th minus the 5th, and the average day's highest hour minus its lowest, on a log scale.

Price by hour of day

The daily price shape by year: how deep the midday trough has become and how sharp the evening peak. One line per year. The median keeps a few extreme days (Winter Storm Uri in 2021) from lifting a whole year; the 5th percentile shows the shape of a cheap day.
