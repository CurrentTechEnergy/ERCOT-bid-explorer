Orange Grove Solar OGS_SLR_UNIT1

Orange Grove Solar (OGS_SLR_UNIT1) is a 130 MW solar farm in ERCOT, in Jim Wells County, South zone, in service since 2025. Its offers are decided by Enbridge Solar LLC and scheduled by Tenaska Power Services Co. Public buyers of its output: AT&T.
