South Trent Wind STWF_T1

South Trent Wind (STWF_T1) is a 94 MW wind farm in ERCOT, in Nolan County, West zone, in service since 2009. Its offers are decided by South Trent Wind LLC and scheduled by American Electric Power Service Corp. Public buyers of its output: AEP Energy Partners.
