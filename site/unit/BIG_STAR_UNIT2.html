Big Star Solar U2 BIG_STAR_UNIT2

Big Star Solar U2 (BIG_STAR_UNIT2) is a 70 MW solar farm in ERCOT, in Bastrop County, South zone, in service since 2025. Its offers are decided by Rwe Clean Energy LLC and scheduled by Rwe Americas Qse LLC. Owned by Big Star Solar LLC. Public buyers of its output: Best Buy, Constellation, McCormick & Company, PPG Industries, PepsiCo, ViacomCBS (KTVT and KTXA TV stations).
