Cactus Flats Wind U1 CFLATS_U1

Cactus Flats Wind U1 (CFLATS_U1) is a 142 MW wind farm in ERCOT, in Concho County, West zone, in service since 2018. Its offers are decided by SP Cactus Flats Wind Energy LLC and scheduled by Southern Power Company. Public buyers of its output: General Mills, General Motors.
