Samson Solar 3 U2 SAMSON_3_G2

Samson Solar 3 U2 (SAMSON_3_G2) is a 62 MW solar farm in ERCOT, in Lamar County, North zone, in service since 2025. Its offers are decided and scheduled by Samson Solar Energy III LLC. Public buyers of its output: AT&T, Bryan Texas Utilities, City of Denton, Garland Power & Light, Google, Home Depot, Honda, McDonald's, Texas A&M University.
