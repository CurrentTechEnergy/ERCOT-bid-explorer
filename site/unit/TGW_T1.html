Gulf Wind I TGW_T1

Gulf Wind I (TGW_T1) is a 135 MW wind farm in ERCOT, in Kenedy County, Coastal zone, in service since 2008. Its offers are decided by Pattern Gulf Wind LLC and scheduled by Tenaska Power Services Co. Public buyers of its output: Austin Energy. How it offers into ERCOT, how much is curtailed at low prices, and the hedge signals in its bids.
