Privacy

The site has no accounts, sets no cookies and shows no ads.

  • Page counts. GoatCounter counts page views. It sets no cookies and keeps no personal data.
  • Unit email alerts. If you follow a unit, we keep your email address, the unit and the dates you signed up and confirmed, in a database on Cloudflare. Emails are sent through Resend. Nothing is sent until you confirm, unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 14 days, and every email has links to stop following one unit or everything, which deletes the record. Your address is used only to send those alerts.
  • Sign-up limits. To stop abuse, the alert server counts sign-ups per IP address and confirmation emails per address for one day, then deletes the counts.
  • Contact form. Messages go through Formspree to the person who runs the site, and are used only to reply.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the alert server on Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may log IP addresses for security and operations under their own policies.

We don't sell or share your information. To have your details deleted, or for any question, use the contact form.
