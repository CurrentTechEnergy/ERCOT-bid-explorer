Floor-priced MW by technology

Units online

Average number of units with an online status each day. Each combined-cycle configuration is reported as its own resource, so combined-cycle counts are configurations, not plants.

Units online vs price

One dot per day. The line is the median for each $5 band of price. Commitment decisions are made ahead of time, so the trailing week's price often explains them better than the same day's.

Units online for only part of the day

Units online between 1 and 22 hours of the day: a measure of cycling and two-shifting. It cannot yet tell a unit that shut down overnight and restarted from one that started once and stayed on, and peaking turbines that run a few hours count too.
