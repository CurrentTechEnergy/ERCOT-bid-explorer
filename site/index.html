    Who is expected to run, by how often it turns off

    Each bar is a group's full capacity. Dark is minimum output that runs at any price, the tick is expected output, light is expected off, hatched is on outage.

    How it's built

    Thermal units are grouped by how often they have turned off on price. Daily units are called from what staying on through the day's cheap stretch would cost them at their own node against what they have walked away from before; seasonal and multi-day units from the month's price level; peakers from how often they ran at this price and hour. Grey is units with too few days to call. Nuclear sits in the always-on group, which is off only for outages, never on price. Never-on units had no running hour in the 60-day history and are held off.

    Who is expected to run, by technology

    The same units by technology; hover a bar for the calls behind it. Wind and solar run to nameplate: solid is expected output, lighter is curtailed on price, hatched is not available from the weather.

    Who was on, by the 2-day fit

    Which units must have been committed to produce ERCOT's aggregate thermal offer curve, two days after the day. The fit starts from the forecast's set and flips units on or off until the curve matches. An inference, not a published actual: the 60-day disclosure gives the answer. Each bar is the group's full capacity; dark is the fitted set's minimum output, light its maximum, and the tick is the forecast's maximum.

    How it's built

    The 2-day SCED energy curves report sums every non-IRR unit's offer curve for every SCED run. The MW offered at or below the floor and $0 are mostly the minimum output of the units that were on, and the MW at $10, $25, $50 and the cap say which of them it was. Units are tried off, on (in each combined-cycle configuration) and on outside their forecast off window, in order of how uncertain the forecast was about them, and a change is kept when it brings the fleet curve closer to ERCOT's. Nuclear, hydro and other are fixed; peakers carry one online share per hour.
