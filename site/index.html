    Who is expected to run, by how often it turns off

    Each bar is a group's full capacity. Dark is minimum output that runs at any price, the tick is expected output, light is expected off, hatched is on outage.

    How it's built

    Thermal units are grouped by how often they have turned off on price. Daily units are called from what staying on through the day's cheap stretch would cost them at their own node against what they have walked away from before; seasonal and multi-day units from the month's price level; peakers from how often they ran at this price and hour. Grey is units with too few days to call. Nuclear sits in the never-off group.

    Who is expected to run, by technology

    The same units by technology; hover a bar for the calls behind it. Wind and solar run to nameplate: solid is expected output, lighter is curtailed on price, hatched is not available from the weather.
