Revenue by month?

Wind and solar revenue by month, day-ahead awards plus real-time at each unit's node. Hover a month for $/MWh. Curtailment is on Renewables.

How it's built

Energy revenue on the two-settlement basis, whatever the controls above say: the day-ahead award at the DA price plus the real-time deviation at the unit's node where it has day-ahead data, real-time at its node otherwise (most wind and solar), and system lambda where the node price is missing; plus ancillary services. Curtailed energy and its worth at the node are from the curtailment panel on Renewables. Hover a month for the split.

Realised $/MWh by unit ?

Each wind and solar unit's revenue per MWh produced, with its curtailed share. Dashed lines are each technology's median. Click a unit to open it.

How it's built

Revenue as above over the unit's energy, for every wind and solar unit over every loaded 60-day day. The dashed lines are each technology's median. Curtailed share is the unit's curtailed energy (HSL minus base point, hour by hour) over its available energy, the same figure as "Of available" in the curtailment table on Renewables. Click a column to sort and a unit to open its page.

Source: ERCOT EMIL products NP3-965-ER (60-Day SCED Disclosure), NP3-966-ER (60-Day DAM Disclosure), NP6-905-CD (Settlement Point Prices) and NP6-322-CD (SCED System Lambda); Henry Hub spot price from the EIA; Houston Ship Channel and Waha daily prices from OilPriceAPI. Built by the data update workflow from the days already loaded; the 60-day reports run about two months behind.
