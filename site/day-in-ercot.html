    Online minimum and maximum, by hour

    How much of each group could have come off in each hour without a shutdown. The solid bar is the online units' summed minimum output (LSL, the lowest they can run while online); the light bar runs up to their summed maximum (HSL); the dark line is what they ran. Where the line sits on the solid bar, the group was at its floor and could only cut further by turning units off. In the "by how often it turns off" view, peakers get their own chart. Wind and solar show HSL only. Shaded hours: real-time system lambda at $10/MWh or less. Each chart has its own scale; click an hour to pick it for the panels below.

    System floor

    The least the online thermal fleet could run in each hour: every coal, combined-cycle, gas steam and combustion turbine unit online at its LSL, on top of nuclear at its output. Each unit counts for the share of the hour's SCED runs it was online and not starting up or shutting down, so a unit on for 20 minutes adds a third of its LSL. It is the same system floor the Thermal dispatch stack on Real-time offers draws. The dark line is what those units ran. The outlined bar is the day's lowest fossil hour, the hour the Daily minimum floor on Turn-ons and Turn-offs records for this day.
